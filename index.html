about

I'm a Data Analytics Engineering graduate student at George Mason University, building reproducible data pipelines and analytics tools on top of large public datasets. My work sits at the intersection of data engineering, applied machine learning, and the interface stakeholders actually see — SQL and DuckDB for the pipeline, Python and Scikit-learn for the modeling, Streamlit and Plotly for the story.

Most recently I led analytics delivery for an offline flood-risk decision-support platform covering all 78 municipios of Puerto Rico, integrating ten federal data sources — FEMA, USGS, NWS, NOAA, and ACS Census — into a single 5-stage DuckDB pipeline, presented at STAR-TIDES 2026.
